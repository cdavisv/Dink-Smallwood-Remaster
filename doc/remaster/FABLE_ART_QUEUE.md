# Fable art queue

Art work that needs Blender or new art (meshes, textures, sculpts, rigs). Opus streams do not do these: they find them
and queue them here for the Fable 5.2 art sessions (owner rule: art design only on Fable 5.2). One row per item.
Priority 1 = seen by every player on the story path and clearly wrong; 2 = on the story path, smaller or rarer;
3 = off the path or minor. The evidence path is relative to `DinkRemasterUE/`. Remove a row when its PR merges.
