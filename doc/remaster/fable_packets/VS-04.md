## 1. The 1997 reference

Dink's monsters are drawn in eight directions (sequence = base + direction; 1/3/7/9 diagonal walks are what the
placements use). Brain 9 sprites. The pictures (`dink.ini` lines 575-578, 634-638, 770-772):

- **Bonca** (`foes/bonca`): a squat, hunched reptile-ape on two thick legs with a long tail curled up behind; dark
  brown hide with orange tiger stripes (531), the same body in grey-white stripes (601, `en-bong`, 40 hp) and purple
  stripes (611, `en-pbon`, 30 hp); small white eye, dark red-brown legs. Attack 542/592/622: lunges forward and bites.
- **Slayer** (`foes/slayers`): a long, low, teal-green insectoid with a spiked ridge along the back, thin jointed
  legs and a whip tail longer than its body (641-649); attack 632-638 rears up on the hind legs.
- **Stone giant** (`foes/stonegnt`): a hunched sandstone brute, knuckles on the ground like a gorilla (821-829);
  attack 811-819 swings a rock arm. It has no `base_death`.
- **Fish** (`434`): a small pale blue fish (8 placements, swimming).

## 2. Real-world size

Creatures use the base scale (`tools/analyse/regions.toml` `base_m_per_px = 0.025`; `export_import_bundle.py`
`BASE_SCALE_CATEGORIES` = npc, enemy, animal, pickup, effect): **2.5 cm per 1997 pixel x the placement's size %**.
The walk frames are 77-110 px wide (bonca), 112-183 px (slayer, tail included), 104 x 88 px (stone giant): a bonca
is about **2.0-2.7 m long and 2.3 m high**, a slayer **2.8-4.6 m from head to tail tip**, a stone giant about
**2.6 x 2.2 m** (hunched). Cards in the game today: 1.9-4.5 m wide, 1.8-3.5 m tall (per placement size %). Fit the
mesh to the card height of its placement (`scale` in the class row), not to a real animal.

## 5. What to make

1. **Bonca**: rework `SK_Bonca_PR` toward a believable animal at 1 m: skin with pore and fold detail, scales or
   hide, the dark brown body with orange stripes of 531 (and the grey and purple sets as material instances of the
   same BC, not three meshes), the white eye, red-brown legs; keep the 16-bone rig and the WIP clips (re-export
   if the mesh changes). Make the stripes readable at the play camera.
2. **Slayer**: add the jaw/mouth, segment the legs and back spikes, chitin with wet highlights, teal-green of 641.
3. **Stone giant**: fix the joint smearing (rigid stone plates per bone or a rock shell that hides the bend),
   real eye sockets and mouth, sandstone of 821.
4. **Fish**: optional, small (8 placements); the pale blue fish of 434 and a swim clip, or leave on cards and say so.
5. Goblins are their own packet (GP-Q3) but in the same branch.

## 7. Import and binding

- Rig and clips: `Unclaimed/tools/export_monster_ue.py` (on the WIP branch) exports mesh + clips in the creature
  convention; the kit's own `SK_*_PR_LOD*.fbx` are refused by the creature import gate ("non-unit reference bone scale").
  Import with the branch's `Scripts/import_photoreal_art.py` creature section (390 new lines on the WIP branch; the
  duck/slime/pig part of it lands with PR #113), e.g. `DINK_PHOTOREAL_ONLY=bonca,slayer,stone_giant Scripts/import_photoreal_art.sh`, one
  Unreal step through `unreal_turn.sh`. Then `git add` the packages and `python3 -I Scripts/scrub_private_paths.py --fix
  DinkRemasterUE/Content/Dink/Photoreal/Creatures/<Name>` from the repository root.
- Binding row (`Content/Dink/Data/Art/creatures.json`, list `classes`; format in `IMPORT.md` "Creature classes"):
  ```json
  {"class": "bonca_brown", "mesh": "/Game/Dink/Photoreal/Creatures/<Name>/SK_<Name>_PR.SK_<Name>_PR",
   "locomotion": {"idle": "<AN_Idle>", "gaits": [{"clip": "<AN_Walk>", "speed_cm_s": <from clips.json>}, {"clip": "<AN_Run>", "speed_cm_s": <...>}]},
   "roles": {"walk": {"sequences": [531, 533, 537, 539], "locomotion": true}, "attack": {"sequences": [541, 543, 547, 549], "clip": "<AN_Attack>"}},
   "corpses": [{"sequences": [551, 553, 557, 559], "mesh": "/Game/Dink/Photoreal/Creatures/<Name>/SM_<Name>_Corpse_PR.SM_<Name>_Corpse_PR",
                "decal": {"material": "<pool decal MI>", "size_cm": [<along>, <across>], "depth_cm": 45}}],
   "materials": {"0": "<MI per colour variant>"}, "scale": <fit to the 1997 card>, "note": "<1997 citation>"}
  ```
  The WIP branch already has these rows: correct them, do not start new ones. A row that names a missing asset binds
  nothing (warning `DINK CREATURE CLASS` in the log) and the sprites keep their cards.
- Coverage: `art_coverage.py` counts class-bound placements `final` (`class-bound:` evidence).

## 8. Prove it in the game

From `DinkRemasterUE/` of your private copy (offline, writes two scripts):
```bash
python3 -I Scripts/CreatureQA/make_scripts.py --out Saved/PhotorealPass/fable/VS-04 --name monsters \
    --play 29:300:90 --close 29:300:90 --kill 29:300:90 --play 90:210:198 --play 157:216:84 --kill 157:216:84 --play 178:324:84 --kill 178:324:84
# then ONE Unreal step per call, through the lock of the shared checkout:
<shared>/DinkRemasterUE/Saved/Gate2Coord/unreal_turn.sh Scripts/PhotorealQA/run_capture.sh "$PWD/Saved/PhotorealPass/fable/VS-04/monsters_after.txt"
<shared>/DinkRemasterUE/Saved/Gate2Coord/unreal_turn.sh Scripts/PhotorealQA/run_capture.sh "$PWD/Saved/PhotorealPass/fable/VS-04/monsters_before.txt" -dpcvars=dink.CreatureClassArt=0
```
The `<screen>:<x>:<y>` points are walkable map points (hardness 0 in a 17 px square) computed for this packet from
`Content/Dink/Data/Gameplay/Hardness`; `--kill` swings at whatever comes at the player with strength 60. Sheets: `Scripts/CreatureQA/sheet.py` (see `Scripts/CreatureQA/README.md`).

Screens: 29 (brown boncas, `en-bonc`), 90 (grey, `s7-bong`), 157 (slayers, `s7-slay`), 178 (stone giants, `en-stg`). For the purple bonca add `--play 514:<x>:<y>`.

Then, before the PR (each Unreal step through `unreal_turn.sh`): `Scripts/test.sh Dink.Art`, the probe `Scripts/test.sh Dink.Gameplay.Probe` (the twenty md5s of
`Plugins/DinkParity/Docs/packaged_probe.md` must stay equal), `python3 -m unittest discover -s Scripts/tests`,
`python3 -I Scripts/art_coverage.py` before/after, and look at every frame yourself, at the play camera and close up.
