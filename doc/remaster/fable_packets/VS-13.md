## 4. Start from (existing sources, half-done work)

- Light: `environment.json` `indoor` looks (`dungeon`: lamps, fill, ambient, `IndoorEV`), console `dink.IndoorLight`,
  `dink.IndoorEV`; round 7 interior light values (IMPORT.md "Interior light").
- Walls: `WallKits/` (dungeon straight A/B, curve, half, arch, breach, ivy; `WallKits.blend`), terrain sets
  `CaveRock`, `CaveFloor`, `DungeonMasonry(Damp)`, `DungeonFlagstone`; props kit (torches, bones, rubble).

## 5. What to make

First (no Blender, any stream): raise the dungeon light so frames read (target mean luma about 0.2 like the interiors). Then (Fable): wall variety (damp streaks, moss, rubble at the foot), cave rock that is not a grey plane, props where 1997 has them.
