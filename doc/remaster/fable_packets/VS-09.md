## 1. The 1997 reference

1997 grounds are 50 px tiles (grass, dirt, sand, path) with hand-drawn transition tiles; the remaster turns tile classes into terrain-layer masks (`terrain_mask()`), keeping the tile rectangles.

## 2. Real-world size

A tile is 50 px: 3.4 m (wilderness), 5 m (settlement, 10 cm/px).

## 3. Where it shows

117 screens graded "rect patches" / "tile-shaped" in the visual sweep, worst: 059-063, 122, 245-249, 306, 310, 317, 318, 341, 383, 384, 414-416, 430, 489, 694, 726-733.

Before frames: `VisualSweep_15Nov2026/frames/059_*.jpg`, `245_*`, `341_*`, `383_*`, `sheets/over_12.jpg`, `over_19.jpg`; the full list is the per-screen table of `VisualSweep_15Nov2026/README.md`.

## 4. Start from (existing sources, half-done work)

- `Scripts/export_import_bundle.py` `terrain_mask()` / `fold_terrain_layers()` and the material `M_Env_Terrain`
  (`Scripts/import_environment_materials.py`, `Environment/terrain_layers.json`). The water banks (#126) are the model:
  bent, noise-perturbed outlines and a distance field in the mask.
- `art/ground-parallax-relief` (local worktree `DinkGroundRelief`, 3 WIP commits; GitHub backup
  `wip-backup/14Nov/art/ground-parallax-relief`): POM relief in `M_Env_Terrain` from the H maps; rebuilds the same
  material: rebase one onto the other.

## 5. What to make

Per layer boundary: replace tile-rectangle edges by noise-bent, feathered outlines (distance field + height-based blend), keep paths continuous across screen rims (WB-1's cross-border mask), keep every hardness-driven edge (cliffs, water) where it is.
