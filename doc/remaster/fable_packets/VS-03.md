## 1. The 1997 reference

`s7-drag`, `sc-drag`, `en-drag` (brain 10, 80 hp) walk 202-208 (a winged, bat-winged brown dragon with green-grey
membrane wings raised, long tail; it spits fire while it walks, there is no attack sequence) and set
`sp_base_death(210)`. Only 212 and 216 exist: `add_kill_sprite` mirrors 4 -> 6 and 8 -> 2, so a killed dragon
leaves **212** (torn: head, neck and one wing, raw red flesh) or **216** (torn body with a wing), until the screen changes.

## 5. What to make

Wings with bone fingers and thin translucent membrane (two-sided, subsurface), an opening jaw with teeth and a tongue,
scale detail, the brown body and green-grey wings of 202; a fire-breath pose or clip is optional (the fireball is a
script sprite, GP-Q9). For the kill: scale the corpse row so the remains are as large against the live dragon as
212/216 are against 202; use the 212 remains (GP-Q7) for directions 2 and 8, 216 for 4 and 6.

## 6. Gore bar (owner rule: at least the 1997 level, never less)

212/216: the dragon is **torn apart**: a severed head and neck or a ripped torso with one wing, **raw red flesh at the
tears, red pooling under it**, red dots around. The remains are about two thirds of the live dragon's width. Bar:
torn (not intact), red flesh visible from the play camera, pool and dots, remains not smaller than that ratio.

## 7. Import and binding

- Rig and clips: `Unclaimed/tools/export_monster_ue.py` (on the WIP branch) exports mesh + clips in the creature
  convention; the kit's own `SK_*_PR_LOD*.fbx` are refused by the creature import gate ("non-unit reference bone scale").
  Import with the branch's `Scripts/import_photoreal_art.py` creature section (390 new lines on the WIP branch; the
  duck/slime/pig part of it lands with PR #113), e.g. `DINK_PHOTOREAL_ONLY=dragon Scripts/import_photoreal_art.sh`, one
  Unreal step through `unreal_turn.sh`. Then `git add` the packages and `python3 -I Scripts/scrub_private_paths.py --fix
  DinkRemasterUE/Content/Dink/Photoreal/Creatures/<Name>` from the repository root.
- Binding row (`Content/Dink/Data/Art/creatures.json`, list `classes`; format in `IMPORT.md` "Creature classes"):
  ```json
  {"class": "dragon", "mesh": "/Game/Dink/Photoreal/Creatures/<Name>/SK_<Name>_PR.SK_<Name>_PR",
   "locomotion": {"idle": "<AN_Idle>", "gaits": [{"clip": "<AN_Walk>", "speed_cm_s": <from clips.json>}, {"clip": "<AN_Run>", "speed_cm_s": <...>}]},
   "roles": {"walk": {"sequences": [201, 202, 203, 204, 206, 207, 208, 209], "locomotion": true}},
   "corpses": [{"sequences": [211, 212, 213, 214, 216, 217, 218, 219], "mesh": "/Game/Dink/Photoreal/Creatures/<Name>/SM_<Name>_Corpse_PR.SM_<Name>_Corpse_PR",
                "decal": {"material": "<pool decal MI>", "size_cm": [<along>, <across>], "depth_cm": 45}}],
   "materials": {"0": "<MI per colour variant>"}, "scale": <fit to the 1997 card>, "note": "<1997 citation>"}
  ```

  nothing (warning `DINK CREATURE CLASS` in the log) and the sprites keep their cards.

## 8. Prove it in the game

From `DinkRemasterUE/` of your private copy (offline, writes two scripts):
```bash
python3 -I Scripts/CreatureQA/make_scripts.py --out Saved/PhotorealPass/fable/VS-03 --name dragon \
    --play 124:306:168 --close 124:306:168 --kill 124:306:168 --play 159:258:144
# then ONE Unreal step per call, through the lock of the shared checkout:
<shared>/DinkRemasterUE/Saved/Gate2Coord/unreal_turn.sh Scripts/PhotorealQA/run_capture.sh "$PWD/Saved/PhotorealPass/fable/VS-03/dragon_after.txt"
<shared>/DinkRemasterUE/Saved/Gate2Coord/unreal_turn.sh Scripts/PhotorealQA/run_capture.sh "$PWD/Saved/PhotorealPass/fable/VS-03/dragon_before.txt" -dpcvars=dink.CreatureClassArt=0
```
The `<screen>:<x>:<y>` points are walkable map points (hardness 0 in a 17 px square) computed for this packet from
`Content/Dink/Data/Gameplay/Hardness`; `--kill` swings at whatever comes at the player with strength 60. Sheets: `Scripts/CreatureQA/sheet.py` (see `Scripts/CreatureQA/README.md`).

Then, before the PR (each Unreal step through `unreal_turn.sh`): `Scripts/test.sh Dink.Art`, the probe `Scripts/test.sh Dink.Gameplay.Probe` (the twenty md5s of
`Plugins/DinkParity/Docs/packaged_probe.md` must stay equal), `python3 -m unittest discover -s Scripts/tests`,
`python3 -I Scripts/art_coverage.py` before/after, and look at every frame yourself, at the play camera and close up.
