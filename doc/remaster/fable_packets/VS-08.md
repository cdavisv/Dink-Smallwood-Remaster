## 5. What to make

A hole needs depth: either a pit mesh whose rim sits on the ground and whose throat goes down with a black falloff
(with the terrain or a masked decal hiding the ground over it), or a parallax/POM decal material with a dark gradient
and rim relief. Cave mouths: rock rim with a shaft going down (reuse `SM_Cave_Hole_PR`'s shoring). Cracks: a narrow
trench with depth, not a flat dark shape. Keep the warp touch box and hardness untouched.
