# Broken barrel V4 — curved coopered source study

Open `PhotorealBrokenBarrelV4_CurvedSource.blend`; the proposed editable collection is `V4_CooperedFragments_Editable`. It contains18 curved stave/splinter fragments, four bent thin iron straps and three small split head-board pieces. All earlier V3/export collections and the metric comparison are hidden archives, not additional proposed geometry. No runtime asset/binding is changed.

The new staves are solids sampled from a coopered barrel shell:0.42m bilge radius,0.35m end radius over0.98m stave height, nominal18-stave angular width,23–25mm thickness. Fragments retain the shell's longitudinal bilge bow and transverse cup; some lie concave-up. Fractures vary across the width AND through thickness, with narrower grain-following split pieces. Tiny longitudinal edge chamfers catch light without flattening the broken caps. Legitimately flat head-board fragments are distinct from the curved staves. Iron is a2.2mm-thick/37mm-wide twisted strip with solid cut ends and varying open arcs, not a torus.

Original173/frame6 guides the low radial scatter and wood/iron identity rather than its pixels. Final scatter is approximately0.99×1.22×0.21m, comparable to V3's intended footprint and the original intact barrel0.84m diameter/1.01m high. Each stave is rigidly oriented to level its end chord while retaining its actual curved geometry; the geometry is never vertically compressed. Contact-height placement is a source composition aid, not a physics simulation or proof that every surface is collision-free.
