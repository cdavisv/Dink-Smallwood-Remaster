NormalXY attenuated.7;90micrometre authored periodic porous height added, normalized; shaderNormalMap1/OpenGL+Y/nativegreenfliptrue. No mortar/joints are baked onto an individual modeled brick.

FiredClayMasonry2m is a separate generic2m continuous-wall set, derived from that clay plus registered lime mortar. Ninebricks across/even28courses, halfstagger,8mmjoints; physicalbrickface214.22x63.43mm. Evenrows and integerpatchperiods preserve tiling. This role is used on unchanged continuous chimney geometry, not individual brick cells.
