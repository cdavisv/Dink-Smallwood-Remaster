# Starting cottage shared exterior source

This source derives from the project's authored cottage and the reviewed House439 exterior V6 source. `Provenance/ReviewedGeometry_Source.blend` is the exact packed reviewed donor (SHA256 30dc4b7705941adde67622f7f7f2a47b96edf5fc0085646a18382dffef87d824). Historical reviewed receipts retain their original paths, hashes and limitations. The included donor allows the source adaptation recipe to run without the old QA checkout. No generated direction-image pixels or downloaded third-party mesh is used.

The six opaque surface graphs and pixels are byte/graph-identical to the reviewed candidate, with shared names and paths normalized. Licensed photographic maps and their CC0 publisher receipts are registered in SharedMaterials/ArchitecturalSurfaces2mV1/PROVENANCE.md. Artist-authored surfaces remain qualified as authored, not measured scans. The glass is the separately registered procedural ThinWavyGlass2mV1 derivative; its thin-wall UV mapping replaces the historical world-position/volumetric shader explicitly. It is not optical parity with volumetric source glass.

All 319 source mesh objects preserve positions, topology, existing UVs, corner normals, vertex colours, transforms and material semantics exactly through save/reopen. The actual joined FBX has 194192 triangles and the six original collision hulls. Explicit post-join corner normals are restored before export; linear SharedSurfaceData must be serialized as Color0. Unused source material slots are removed only from the disposable joined export, without changing per-face roles. The source remains unjoined to preserve editable construction objects.

Original identity is the cottage sprite sequence63/frame1, observed on screens439/530/617. The original artwork includes a separate door (home02) and chimney (home11) guide, not additional globally replaced sprite families. This registration does not fan out to other frames of sequence63, update families.json, or activate Content/Data replacements. The 194192-triangle building exceeds the 60000 soft target but remains below the 200000 hard limit; this native trial preserves the reviewed full cottage construction, roof dressing, windows and metalwork. Performance/cooked collision remain unproven.

Glass UV correction: whole-object bounds were mistakenly interpreted as glass texel scale. Actual per-glass-triangle Jacobians show main panes exactly2m/UV, areaweighted about2.0045m/UV, with small bevel anisotropy up to1.128 and no degenerateUVs. Coordinating owner accepted KEEP originalUV0/sampler0 and no redundantUV2. Closed12mm panes, one-sided native thin-sheet shading, rootOpacity0/surfaceCoverage1 are approved for a game trial only. Source CPU glass remains a thin-wall shader applied to closed geometry. Actual outside/inside/backdrop/grazing-reflection native proof is required before final optical acceptance.

Three fixed matched source renders are inspected. Dark interior makes window panes look dark and softly reflective; the cottage closeup does not independently prove transmission. The standalone shared glass reuse scene shows a patterned backdrop through the surface. Neither source view is final native appearance approval. No UE execution, game import, runtime mutation or final all-assets completion is claimed.
