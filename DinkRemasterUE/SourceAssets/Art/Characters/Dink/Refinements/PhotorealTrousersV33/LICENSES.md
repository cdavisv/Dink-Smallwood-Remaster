# Provenance and licences

The new cloth construction, fitted geometry, procedural textile/stitch source and artwork were authored for this Dink remaster project. They are source contributions under the repository's applicable project terms; this packet does not declare a new blanket licence for the complete character.

`Provenance/makehuman_sources.json`, `MakeHuman_CC0.txt` and `MPFB_LICENSE.md` record the MakeHuman system-data source and CC0 provenance used for the character context. MPFB authoring software is separately GPLv3; its software licence is not a blanket GPL claim for exported artwork. The reference character's exact rig names/rest metadata are preserved for compatibility; no Epic reference robot geometry is included in the delivered meshes.

Original Dink images in `References/` are project reference/provenance material, governed by their existing upstream/repository rights, not relabelled as new CC0 textures. `REFERENCE_INDEX.json` and original-sequence provenance identify their bytes and origin. They guide silhouette/palette/red armband placement and are not baked into the new textile maps. A separate backpack remains an earlier remaster interpretation, not a conclusively identified original sprite feature.

Copied historical guides retain their original content and provenance index. Native material graphs remain proposals; no third-party paid generation was invoked for this packet.
