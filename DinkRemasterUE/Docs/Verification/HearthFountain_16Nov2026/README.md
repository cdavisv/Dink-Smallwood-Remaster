## Still below the bar

- The fountain's spray is narrower and paler than 1997's broad blue umbrella, and faint in daylight; nothing splashes in
  the basin and the water's surface does not move.
- Effect cards are unlit: the spray glows a little in the dungeon; the hearth fire does not darken with the room.
- The hearth of 163 is sized for the fieldstone fireplace of #194 (row `offset_cm`: lifted 30 cm, 1.5 times): without
  that fireplace it would stand too tall on the 1997 picture.
- Two rounds before this were wrong and are not shown: the first spray was 520 big drops that drew as white blobs, and
  the first hearth stood small on a straight glowing foot.
