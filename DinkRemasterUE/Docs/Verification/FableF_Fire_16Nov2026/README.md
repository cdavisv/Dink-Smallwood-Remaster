## Still below the bar

- Each fire is one camera-facing card: from the side or from above it is a sheet, and it does not wrap what it burns.
- The burn patches (155, 156, 157) read as cracked, glowing blotches stuck on a camera-facing card, not as charred
  thatch or timber: they need decals on the surface. The thatch under the roof fires is not charred.
- The 1997 burnt hole in the roof is not part of this change (the damage decals of screen 439 are another stream's and
  lie on the grass north of the cottage for the same reason the fires did).
- The fire lights have no shadows; the smoke columns end 7 m up.
